## 10. Company/Security Separation

The design preserves `COMPANY != SECURITY/LISTING`.

Company-level observations include examples such as revenue, EBIT, EBITDA, net income, operating cash flow, free cash flow, total assets, debt, cash, equity and model-input components.

Security/listing-level attributes include ISIN, MIC, canonical primary ticker, share class, price, security-specific shares where applicable, market capitalization, liquidity and price-dependent valuation inputs.

Company fundamentals may be related to multiple securities only through an explicit Company-to-Security relationship. No blind duplication across listings/classes is permitted.

## 16. Raw Storage Modes

Two mandatory modes:

### MODE A — RAW PERSISTENCE PERMITTED

When future entitlement evidence permits persistent raw storage:

- raw provider payload/records may be retained in approved secure storage;
- retention class and term are explicit;
- Git/public repositories remain prohibited;
- replay/reproducibility may use retained raw artifacts.

### MODE B — RAW PERSISTENCE RESTRICTED

When raw persistence is restricted:

- provider payload is transient/minimal;
- only permitted canonical observations and metadata are retained;
- provenance, hashes/manifests and evidence references are kept only where permitted;
- no indefinite raw archive is assumed;
- replay capability is explicitly reduced.

Current conservative default: **MODE B until V3 is resolved**.

## 20. PIT Governance

Required PIT levels:

- `PIT_LEVEL_0_CURRENT_ONLY`
- `PIT_LEVEL_1_PERIOD_HISTORICAL`
- `PIT_LEVEL_2_FILING_DATE_AWARE`
- `PIT_LEVEL_3_VERSIONED_AS_KNOWN`

Interpretation:

- Level 0: current provider state only;
- Level 1: historical fiscal periods available;
- Level 2: filing/availability metadata supports anti-look-ahead control;
- Level 3: historical versions/restatements reconstruct true as-known-at-time state.

**TRUE PIT CAPABILITY: NOT YET PROVEN**

The design may initially use Levels 0–2 only where evidence supports them. Level 3 is prohibited until future evidence/data proves it.
